Old world craft,
made to be felt.

From repurposing and revitalizing timeless pieces to creating unique "moments" that provoke emotion and inspire the senses, NonConventionalKrafts blurs the line between structure and art. Working primarily in wood, with steel and concrete as counterpoint, every piece begins as a conversation about how a space should feel — then gets built to last.

Material Palette

Wood remembers. We just help it speak.

Every material is chosen for what it can still become — steel and concrete step in only when a piece asks for their weight.

MAT / 01 Primary

Wood

Domestic hardwoods and reclaimed timber, chosen for the story already in the grain. It's where every piece starts — and usually where it's still speaking loudest when it's finished.

MAT / 02 Secondary

Steel

Blackened and raw steel, brought in to give a piece its edge — literally and otherwise. A quiet nod to the trade this practice was built from.

MAT / 03 Secondary

Concrete

Cast and finished by hand, for the pieces that need mass and texture underneath them — grounded, a little raw, never an afterthought.

How a Piece Comes Together

Slow, by hand, on purpose

Every commission moves through the same unhurried sequence, start to finish, in the Solana Beach shop.

Listen & Imagine

We talk through the space, how it's lived in, and what it's missing — sketches and material studies before anything is cut.

Shape & Build

Milling, joinery, welding, and casting, done by hand, with the grain and texture of each material leading the way.

Finish & Place

Final finishing and installation, so the piece arrives ready to become part of the room it was made for.

Get In Touch

Let's make a moment

Tell me about the space, and what you want it to feel like. Most pieces start as nothing more than a sketch and a conversation about materials.
